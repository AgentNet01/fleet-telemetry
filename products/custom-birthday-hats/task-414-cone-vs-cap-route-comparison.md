## Recommendation (informs, does not decide)
**Lead with the embroidered cap.** It is the only route with a complete listing package, a computed margin (39.2%), a POD maker with no inventory, and the highest perceived value — the gift-worthy hat, not the party-supply hat. The premium-vs-market risk ($32.99 above 12/14 comps) is real but manageable for gift-driven buyers.

**Keep the cone as the follow-up lane** — its in-house POD route is the fastest path to a first sale if the PM wants revenue sooner, and its $1.40 materials cost leaves room for a $12–$16 budget price. But it needs cone listing copy (title/tags/description) written first, and its per-order labor caps scale.

**What the PM must decide:** (1) which route leads, (2) cone listing copy if the cone leads or follows, (3) Printify account go-ahead if the cap leads. The cap package (#342/#343/#344) is ready the moment the PM picks it; the cone route needs copy before it can list.
