# Cone party hat maker research — POD/supplier options (task #327)

**Product:** Custom birthday hats · **Role:** engineer · **Date:** 2026-10-14
Goal: 2–3 maker options for custom printed cone party hats (cardstock, per #78 designs) with unit costs, MOQs, production times, US shipping. Prices below are from public listings 2026-10-14 unless marked ESTIMATE. No supplier has been contacted; quotes are the PM's next step.

## The key finding
No verified US print-on-demand supplier offers custom cone party hats (Printify/Printful do caps, not cone hats). The US results are retailers/competitors, not suppliers (Big Dot of Happiness: made-in-USA custom photo cone hats, $15.99/set of 8 — a comp, not a maker). So the real options are: China factory bulk, in-house print-on-demand, or a US commercial printer (quote needed).

## Option A — China factory bulk (Alibaba / Global Sources)
- **Unit cost:** $0.26–$0.50/hat (Qingzhou Hongtu $0.26–0.36, MOQ 1000; Fujian Reeying $0.50, MOQ 1000; Guangzhou Lingcai $0.05–0.07 at 10,000+)
- **MOQ:** 1,000 units per design (some suppliers 500)
- **Production:** 7–35 days (sample 2–9 days; mass production 20–35 days at Reeying, 7–15 days at Lingcai)
- **US shipping:** DHL/FedEx air or sea freight; air adds roughly $1–2/hat on small cartons (ESTIMATE — get a landed quote), sea is cheaper but 30–45 days
- **Fit:** pre-made designs only — a 1,000-unit run cannot be personalized per buyer. Works for the six #78 designs as fixed designs (no photo swap), or as blank stock for overprinting.
- **Conflict with Jack's rule:** "nothing is bought before a customer pays — no samples, no stock" — a 1,000-unit run violates this unless the PM explicitly approves a stock exception.

## Option B — In-house print-on-demand (RECOMMENDED for launch)
- **Unit cost:** ~$1.40 materials ESTIMATE (cardstock $0.30 + ink/toner $0.50 + elastic/pom $0.20 + packaging $0.40) + ~10 min labor per hat
- **MOQ:** none — one hat per order
- **Production:** same day (print, cut, roll, attach elastic/pom)
- **US shipping:** USPS Ground Advantage, ~$4–5 ESTIMATE (verify at ship time)
- **Fit:** the ONLY option that supports true per-order photo personalization (buyer photo swapped into the #78 design, printed per order). Fits Jack's no-stock rule exactly. Downside: labor doesn't scale; quality depends on our printer (use a laser printer or a local print shop for color-critical runs).

## Option C — US commercial print shop, short runs (UNVERIFIED — needs quotes)
- **Unit cost:** unknown — request quotes for 50–100 cardstock sheets per design
- **MOQ:** typically 50–100 sheets per design (shop-dependent)
- **Production:** 3–7 business days ESTIMATE (shop-dependent)
- **US shipping:** domestic ground, 2–5 days
- **Fit:** middle ground — a local/online printer (PrintNinja-class) prints the #78 designs on cardstock; we cut, roll, and finish in-house. No supplier has been confirmed to offer cone-hat finishing, so this is a quote-request action, not a ready option. The PM should request 2–3 quotes with a sample sheet.

## Recommendation
Launch on **Option B (in-house POD)** — it is the only route that delivers per-order photo personalization with zero stock, and it keeps unit economics viable at any plausible hat price. Move to **Option A (China bulk)** only for pre-made best-seller designs if volume ever justifies 1,000-unit runs, with an explicit PM stock exception. **Option C** is worth two quote requests as a scale-up hedge.
