# Hats returns and misprint policy one-pager (task #384)

**Product:** Custom birthday hats · **Role:** optimizer · **Date:** 2026-10-14
Per #60 packaging/returns policy. Response SLA: **reply within 1 business day** via Etsy Messages. Log every case in the order record. Applies to both hat routes (embroidered cap: name+age text; cone: photo + text) — the cap has no photo step.

## Final-sale rule
Every hat is made to order with the buyer's name, age, or photo — it cannot be resold. **Hats are final sale: no returns or exchanges for change of mind.** The flows below are the exceptions: embroidery/print errors, damage, and lost packages. Never refuse a remedy in those cases by citing "final sale."

## Misprint / embroidery error
- **Our mistake** (we misspelled a name entered correctly, used the wrong thread colors/design, stitched the wrong age): buyer chooses — **remake rushed and free, or full refund**. No return of the misprinted hat needed.
- **Buyer's typo or wrong photo** (misspelled name in the personalization box, wrong photo uploaded for a cone hat): what the buyer sent is what we make. Buyer must message within **14 days of delivery**; offer a **remake at 50% off**. After 14 days, no remake obligation — seller's discretion.
- Spelling is confirmed at proof approval (where the proof workflow applies) — after approval, a typo is the buyer's.

## Damage in transit
- Buyer reports **within 7 days of delivery** with a photo of the damage AND the packaging it came in.
- **Free replacement** — buyer does not send the damaged hat back.
- If a replacement can't reach the buyer in time (e.g. the event date has passed): **full refund instead**.

## Lost package
- Tracking shows "delivered" but buyer has nothing → ask them to check neighbors, building mail area, porch first. Wait 2 business days for mis-scans, then open a carrier inquiry.
- **No movement 10+ days past estimated delivery** OR **carrier confirms lost** → buyer chooses: **one free reship OR full refund**.
- One free reship max. A second loss → refund.
- If the buyer's event date has passed → refund, don't reship a late personalized hat.
- File the carrier claim; keep the claim number in the order record.

## Cancellation
- **Full refund within 12 hours of purchase.** After production starts, no cancellation (#60).

## Not covered here
- **Etsy cases:** if the buyer opens a case, respond within Etsy's case window; never let a case go unanswered.
- **Chargebacks / second failures on the same order:** PM reviews before the next action.
