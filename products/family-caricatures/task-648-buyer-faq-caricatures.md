### 1. What kind of photo should I upload?
One clear, well-lit photo per person, with every face visible — originals from your camera roll (JPG or PNG), not screenshots. Send photos via Etsy Message after purchase, never in the personalization box. If a photo won't work, we'll ask you for a better one before we start.

### 2. Can I choose the art style?
Yes — the listing offers two art styles to pick at checkout. Samples of both are in the listing images (labeled SAMPLE). Your portrait is drawn in the style you choose.

### 3. How do proofs and revisions work?
You approve a watermarked digital proof before anything is final — nothing delivers until you say so. Your first proof arrives within **3 business days** of your photo. You get **up to 2 free revision rounds** (the PM's decision), each returned within 3 business days. Tell us exactly what to adjust each round. After your 2 free rounds, extra rounds are $10 each.

### 4. How do I receive my caricature?
Digital delivery via Etsy Messages — no shipping, nothing to wait for in the mail. You get high-resolution JPG + PNG files (300 DPI, print-ready up to 16×20"). Print at home or with any print shop; framing is up to you.

### 5. Is my portrait hand-drawn or made with AI?
Your portrait is **AI-assisted digital artwork, custom-made from your photo** — no two are alike. The sample images in the listing are AI-generated previews (labeled SAMPLE/MOCKUP); your caricature is drawn for you from the photo you send.

### 6. How long does the whole thing take?
First proof within 3 business days of your photo. Each revision round takes up to 3 business days. Once you approve, final files arrive within 24 hours. That's the full timeline — we don't promise faster turnarounds.

### 7. Can I order a caricature as a gift?
Absolutely — it's one of the most popular uses. Upload the recipient's photo (with their permission), approve the proof yourself, and gift the digital files — or print and frame it first. Because delivery is digital, there's no shipping delay to worry about.

### 8. What if I don't like the first proof?
That's what the revision rounds are for — most portraits land within the 2 free rounds. Message us with specifics (bigger smile, different background, adjust a feature) and we'll redraw. Nothing is final until you approve it.

### 9. Can I get a refund?
Each portrait is custom-made from your photo and can't be resold, so all sales are final. But if **we** make an error — wrong people, wrong style, a mistake against your approved photo — we'll fix it free or refund you in full. Message us within 7 days of delivery with the issue.
