# Social proof seeding plan: family caricatures (task #574)

**Product:** Family caricatures · **Role:** marketer · **Date:** 2026-10-14
Plan to earn the first 5 reviews, Etsy-compliant. Reviews are earned from real buyers — never paid, never conditional.

## The target
**First 5 reviews** before any paid traffic. Social proof is the listing's conversion engine: a caricature buyer is purchasing an artist's eye sight-unseen, and 5 genuine reviews move the listing from "risky" to "trusted."

## Strategy 1: Direct outreach (the launch circle)
- **Who:** Jack's and Darragh's warm contacts — family, friends, colleagues with kids, couples, grandparents. The people most likely to buy a caricature as a gift or keepsake anyway.
- **How:** Personal message (not a mass blast), occasion-led: "We're launching a custom family caricature shop — a hand-drawn-style portrait from your photo. Would love your support as one of our first buyers."
- **What NOT to do:** never ask anyone to buy in exchange for a review, and never ask for a 5-star review. Ask for the purchase; the review ask comes later, after delivery (see timing below).
- **Target:** 5+ launch-circle buyers in week 1–2 after publish.

## Strategy 2: Follow-up timing (every order)
| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Delivery + 2 days | If the buyer messaged positively, thank them and invite a review: "So glad you love it! If you have a moment, a review would mean the world to us as a new shop." |
| Delivery + 5 days | If no review and no complaint: ONE follow-up message, same tone. Then stop — never send a second. |
| Complaint arrives | Resolve first (remake/refund per policy). Never ask for a review on an unresolved order. |
| Negative review posted | Reply publicly within 1 business day, offer the fix. Never ask the buyer to change it in exchange for anything. |

## Etsy-compliant incentives (what's allowed, what's not)
- **FORBIDDEN:** discounts, free items, refunds, or any reward in exchange for a review — including "leave a review and get 10% off." Etsy's policy treats these as review manipulation.
- **FORBIDDEN:** asking specifically for 5 stars, or scripting what the review should say.
- **ALLOWED:** a thank-you note in every order (no review mention, or a neutral "we'd love your feedback" with no reward attached).
- **ALLOWED:** the free revision round — it earns goodwill that converts to reviews on its own, without any conditional offer.
- **ALLOWED:** asking every buyer, equally, for honest feedback after delivery.
- Bottom line: the only incentive is a great portrait and great service. Anything conditional on a review is off the table.

## Tracking
- Log each order: buyer, delivery date, follow-up 1 sent (Y/N), follow-up 2 sent (Y/N), review received (Y/N, stars).
- Weekly: reviews ÷ delivered orders = review rate. Healthy target: 20%+.
- After 5 reviews: the listing has baseline social proof — shift focus to the traffic plan (#465).

## What this plan does NOT do
- Does not buy reviews, trade reviews, or offer anything for reviews.
- Does not ask the launch circle for anything beyond an honest purchase and, later, honest feedback.
- Does not start until the listing publishes (Jack's authorization).
