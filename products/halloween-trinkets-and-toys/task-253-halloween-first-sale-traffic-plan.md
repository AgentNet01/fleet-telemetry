# First-sale traffic plan: Halloween trinkets (task #253)

**Product:** Halloween trinkets and toys · **Role:** marketer · **Date:** 2026-10-13
Goal: the first sale. Clock: **3 days to the Economy order-by cutoff (Oct 16), 8 days to Standard (Oct 21).** After Oct 16, pre-Halloween delivery needs Standard shipping only. The plan is built around that window.

## The situation
- Listing package is ready: title, 13 tags, 4 shapes, $12.99 + $3.99 hero / $9.99 + $3.99 name variant (margins validated in #254).
- Differentiator: photo personalization — **unserved in the top-10 trinket set** (all comps are name-text or bulk). This is the hook.
- Problem: new listing, zero sales, zero reviews — organic Etsy search ranking will be near the bottom against Star Sellers with 13k–66k sales. We cannot win on ranking; we win on differentiation + paid placement + urgency.

## Etsy search
- The title leads with "Personalized Halloween Photo Keychain" — exact-match for the differentiator query. Keep it.
- Tag gaps to test (48h in, if impressions are thin): swap out the weakest of the current 13 for **bat keychain** (12), **custom photo gift** (17), **spooky party favor** (18) — bat/candy-corn shapes are in the listing but "bat keychain" isn't a tag; all three verified ≤20 chars. (Note: "halloween party favor" is 21 chars and CANNOT be used as an Etsy tag.)
- Measure with Etsy's search analytics: impressions → clicks. If CTR < 1% after 200 impressions, the thumbnail (not the tags) is the problem — swap the lead image to the lifestyle shot.

## Ads readiness
- **Etsy Ads (on-site): ON at $3/day, hero listing only**, starting the day the listing goes live. At $12.99 with $6.39 margin, one sale covers two days of spend. Kill rule: $9 spend with zero sales → pause and fix the thumbnail/title before spending more.
- Offsite Ads: **OPT OUT at launch** (fleet standing recommendation for new shops). #254 explicitly did not assume Offsite — a 15%-attributed order drops hero margin to ~$3.85. Opting out protects the thin variant margin. Revisit only if organic velocity stalls.
- No social spend. The 3-day window is too short for organic social to convert.

## Halloween timing
- Oct 13–16: "Order by Oct 16 for Halloween delivery" in the listing + shop announcement. Economy shipping story.
- Oct 17–21: flip messaging to Standard-only ("order by Oct 21, arrives before Halloween" — verified Standard cutoff per #72). Update the listing's shipping profile emphasis; do not promise dates (per #210's no-guarantee rule).
- After Oct 21: pivot to the post-Halloween retheme (#208) — the same keychain line becomes Christmas ornaments.

## 3 actionable steps
1. **Publish today + Etsy Ads $3/day on the hero listing.** Paid placement bridges the zero-sales ranking gap inside the Economy window. Owner: whoever holds the publish gate (Jack's authorization required).
2. **48-hour tag/image check.** If impressions < 200 or CTR < 1%: swap in bat keychain / halloween party favor / custom photo gift tags; if CTR is the problem, lead with the lifestyle image. Owner: marketer.
3. **Run a 10% opening sale through Oct 21 + cutoff messaging.** Sale price $11.69 hero ($11.69 + $3.99 = $15.68 total; margin $5.21, 33.2% — still >30%). The Oct 16/Oct 21 cutoffs create real urgency. On Oct 17, flip to Standard-only messaging. Owner: marketer.
