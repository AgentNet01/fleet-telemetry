# Pricing validation: Halloween photo keychain line (task #254)

**Product:** Halloween trinkets and toys · **Role:** optimizer · **Date:** 2026-10-13
Builds on: launch-concept-and-pricing.md (#71 market/maker scan basis), market-and-maker-scan.md. All unit costs verified live on Printify's public catalog 2026-10-13; Etsy fees are Etsy's published US rates. Fee math below independently recomputed — matches the launch doc.

## COGS (verified)
- SwiftPOD (USA) custom-shape acrylic keychain (UV print): **$3.74/unit** (standard; $2.75 with Printify Premium — not assumed).
- Economy shipping to US buyer: **$4.79** first item (Standard $5.89). Buyer is charged $3.99 — we absorb $0.80, already reflected in margins below.
- **COGS = $3.74 + $4.79 = $8.53.**
- Delivery: ~1.5d production + 4–8 BD Economy = 6–10 BD total. Pre-Halloween cutoff: order by Oct 16 (Economy) / Oct 22 (Standard).

## Against comp prices
- **Bulk tier** ($0.68–$2.50/unit: squishy favors, ghost keychain $2.34+): not our lane. Name-text personalization, classroom volumes, near-zero margins. We don't compete here.
- **Photo-personalized tier** (our lane): character pillow $12.61+, pet blanket $10.08+, gothic portrait $19.95+, photo blanket $28.00+, light-up pumpkin $29.99+. Range $10–$30.
- **Our position:** $12.99 hero sits at the value end of the photo tier — below the $19.95+ custom-art cluster, above bulk — with 37.6% margin intact. Photo personalization is an unserved differentiator in the top-10 trinket set (zero photo comps), so the price buys position, not a price war.
- **$9.99 variant** (26.3% margin) is the entry hook; thin but defensible as a volume driver. Don't discount it further — $8.53 COGS leaves no room.

## Recommendation
**Launch at $12.99 + $3.99 shipping (hero) and $9.99 + $3.99 (name-only variant).** Margins 26–38% hold at verified COGS. Free-shipping test ($14.99 flat) stays phase 2 — margin drops to ~30% and 9 of the 10 top sellers charge shipping. Revisit only if conversion lags: the lever is the mock-up preview workflow (proven by photo-blanket/pumpkin sellers), not the price.

## Caveats
- Printify Premium ($2.75/unit) would add ~$1/order margin if volume justifies the subscription — compute at 50+ orders/month.
- Shipping charged ($3.99) is below Economy cost ($4.79); if SwiftPOD's Economy rate rises, re-derive before adjusting.
- Offsite Ads (15%) not assumed; if an order attributes, hero margin drops to ~$3.85 — still positive.
