**What size is the keychain?**
The acrylic charm is 2.5" × 2.5", with a sturdy 4.7" silver ball chain. It's sized for backpacks, lunchboxes, trick-or-treat bags, and keys — big enough to show off the photo, light enough for a kid to carry.

**What shapes can I choose from?**
Four: Ghost, Pumpkin, Bat, and Candy Corn. Pick your shape as a variation when you order.

**What kind of photo should I send?**
One clear, front-facing photo works best — good lighting, no heavy filters. JPG or PNG, any phone photo is fine. After checkout, send it to us via Etsy message. If your photo won't work well, we'll tell you before we print anything.

**How does the photo swap work?**
Three steps: (1) choose your shape and add a name if you'd like one printed; (2) send your photo via Etsy message after checkout; (3) we send you a preview mock-up for approval, then print and ship. Nothing gets printed until you approve the preview.

**Can I add a name or message?**
Yes — type the name in the personalization box at checkout and we'll print it on the design. Names only, please (no long messages — the charm is 2.5").

**How long does production take?**
We make your keychain to order in 1–2 business days after you approve the preview.

**How long is shipping? Will it arrive before Halloween?**
Shipping options at checkout:
- Economy: 4–8 business days ($3.99)
- Standard: 2–5 business days ($5.89)
Order by **Oct 16** (Economy) or **Oct 22** (Standard) for guaranteed pre-Halloween delivery. We ship from the USA via USPS.

**What's your return policy?**
Because each keychain is personalized and made to order, we can't accept returns — unless we made an error, in which case we'll remake or refund it, your choice. That's why we always send a preview for your approval before printing: what you approve is what we make.

**Is it safe for kids?**
Yes — lightweight acrylic with a smooth finish and a secure ball chain. As with any small item, adult supervision is recommended for children under 3.

**Do you offer bulk / classroom orders?**
Not yet — we're launching with single keychains first. Message us if you're interested in a classroom set and we'll see what we can do.
