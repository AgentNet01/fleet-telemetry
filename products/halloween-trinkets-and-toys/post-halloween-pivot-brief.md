**Winning pattern (observed):** transparent acrylic + buyer photo upload + short optional text (names/year); 50%-off sale pricing on ornaments ($4–$6 landing); keychains at $11–$12 — our $12.99 price point fits the keychain band exactly; size/bow/gift-box variant upsells; Star Seller volume matters more than per-listing reviews (sellers re-list fresh while riding shop-level proof).

**Pivot plan:**
1. First week of November: swap the 4 Halloween shapes for Christmas shapes (tree, snowman, stocking, reindeer) on the same Printify acrylic-keychain pipeline — zero new tooling, same $3.74 COGS, same $12.99 price.
2. Retitle/re-tag for Christmas search ("personalized christmas photo keychain", "stocking stuffer", etc.); reuse the photo-upload workflow and preview-approval process unchanged.
3. Add the observed upsells: gift-box variant, two-sided print option.
4. Christmas order-by dates: ~Dec 15 (Economy) / ~Dec 18 (Standard), published in the listing.
5. Keep the Halloween listing live but de-prioritized (see Angle 2).

## Angle 2 — Early-bird next Halloween (Nov–Oct 2027)

1. **Keep Halloween listings live year-round.** Etsy rewards listing age and accumulated reviews; Halloween search climbs Aug–Sep. Do not delete — just let them idle.
2. **Use Nov–Jul to build the moat:** collect reviews on the Christmas line (same shop = shared Star Seller progress), test which of the 4 Halloween shapes sold best, and develop the classroom bulk kits (task #71's top sellers at $1–$2.50/unit, 66.3k sales).
3. **Switch bulk kits to Alibaba for margin:** custom PVC keychain at $0.11–$0.15/unit (100 MOQ, 4–8 week lead — fine with a spring order) turns the $1–$2.50 classroom tier from operational headache into the highest-margin line.
4. **Re-launch Halloween early September 2027** with reviews banked, bulk tier ready, and Christmas-season shop momentum behind the shop.

## What not to do
- Don't run Halloween ads/copy past Nov 1 — wasted spend; the category goes dormant.
- Don't build new Halloween shapes in Q1 — validate with this year's sales data first.
- Don't drop the photo-personalization workflow — it's the transferable asset across both seasons.
