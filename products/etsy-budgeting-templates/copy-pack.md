## Description
Tired of guessing where your money goes? This all-in-one monthly budget spreadsheet shows you exactly what comes in, what goes out, and what's left — automatically.

No apps, no subscriptions, no spreadsheet skills needed. Open the file, enter your numbers, and the built-in formulas do the rest.

WHAT'S INSIDE — 7 SHEETS
✔ START HERE — step-by-step setup guide so you're budgeting in minutes
✔ Income Log — record every paycheck and income source with date, source, and amount
✔ Expense Log — log every purchase with date, payee, description, a category dropdown, amount, and an essential-spending flag
✔ Categories — 12 budget categories with budgeted vs. auto-actual totals (pulled straight from your expense log), remaining amounts, and % used
✔ Monthly Budget — income, expense, and net totals with a savings target % and an on-track check
✔ Savings Goals — track each goal's target, saved, remaining, % complete, and estimated months to go
✔ Dashboard — auto-calculating overview: totals, net balance, savings rate, biggest spend category, and over-budget count

WHY IT WORKS
✔ Fully auto-calculating dashboard — your numbers update themselves as you log spending
✔ Category budgets pull actuals straight from the expense log — no double entry
✔ Savings-goal tracker with progress and estimated time to reach each goal
✔ Monthly savings-rate target with a clear on-track indicator
✔ Yellow input cells show exactly where to type; formula cells are protected so nothing breaks
✔ Sample rows included — see how it works before you enter your own numbers

WHO IT'S FOR
