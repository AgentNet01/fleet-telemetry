## Concept A — "caricature 15": occupational-caricature budget planner
A digital budget planner template decorated with playful occupational cartoon characters (nurse, firefighter, teacher, police, EMT, etc.), with occupation-aware finance pages (shift differentials, overtime, classroom expenses).

### Buyer
1. **Self-purchase** — shift workers (nurses, firefighters, EMTs, police, teachers) whose irregular pay (overtime, differentials, 10-month teacher pay) makes generic budgets a poor fit; they buy planner/printable products already and respond to identity/pride aesthetics.
2. **Gift purchase** — family/partners buying for a nurse/firefighter. Etsy reviews on occupation products repeatedly mention gifting ("firefighter husband/son", academy graduation gifts); one nurse planner listing explicitly positions itself as "a thoughtful gift for a fellow nurse".

### Value
Occupation-specific financial tooling wrapped in an identity-driven, playful design — more approachable than generic spreadsheets, and personally meaningful enough to gift. Price band observed for occupation-themed planners: ~$1.10–$9.98.

## Concept B — "star map 20": occupational star map budget planner
A digital budget planner template with star-map/constellation-map night-sky aesthetics, occupation-themed (e.g. stethoscope or mortarboard rendered as constellations, "the night you became a nurse" personalization hooks).

### Buyer
1. **Self-purchase** — home-finance organizers drawn to celestial aesthetics for planners they'll actually open; celestial planners with budget pages already exist.
2. **Gift purchase** — gift-givers buying for a milestone moment: nurse graduation/pinning, teacher appreciation. The star-map category on Etsy is overwhelmingly gifting-driven (reviews stress gifting + personalization).

### Value
The proven emotional pull of star maps ("the sky on the night of your milestone," highly personalizable) applied to a practical budgeting template — giftability + utility. Digital star maps cluster at $1.29–$12 (most $5–$8); celestial planners with budget pages sell at $4.40–$7.00.
