# Post-publish first-week monitoring checklist: pet portraits (task #422)

**Product:** Pet portraits · **Role:** optimizer · **Date:** 2026-10-14
Check once daily for the first 7 days after the listing publishes. Each check takes ~15 minutes. Log every day's numbers in one running sheet — never judge a single day in isolation. Thresholds below are this checklist's proposed triggers; they are not borrowed from another product's plan.

## Daily checks (every day, Days 1–7)
- **Views** (Etsy Stats → listing views, last 24h)
- **Favorites** (hearts on the listing)
- **Orders / conversion** (orders ÷ views)
- **Search rank** (Etsy search, logged-out/private window: check 3 of the verified #189 tags; note which page the listing appears on)
- **Messages** (buyer questions, photo issues — reply within 1 business day per #194 templates)
- **Reviews** (only once orders deliver — see review timing below)

## Day-by-day focus
| Day | Focus | What "good" looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Listing indexed | Listing appears in Etsy search for at least 1 verified tag; images, price ($24.99 / $32.99 anchor, 20% opening promo = $19.99 effective per #226), and personalization boxes all live |
| 2 | First traffic | 10+ views; at least 1 favorite or message |
| 3 | Tag health | Listing on pages 1–5 for 3+ verified tags |
| 4 | Conversion signal | 30+ cumulative views; 3+ favorites; or first order |
| 5 | Momentum | Views holding or growing vs Days 2–4; no tag has fallen off the first 5 pages |
| 6 | Promo check | If 0 orders: confirm the 20% opening promo is actually showing ($19.99 effective) |
| 7 | Week-1 verdict | Decide: hold, tweak (tags/images), or escalate to PM (price/promo lever) |

## Thresholds that trigger the traffic-plan levers
| Signal | Threshold | Lever |
|---|---|---|
| Views | <20 views/day by Day 3 | Tag refresh: swap the 2 weakest tags for alternates from #189's research; check the hero image (if CTR is the problem, lead with the before/after sheet from #192) |
| Search rank | Listing NOT on pages 1–5 for 3+ verified tags by Day 4 | Tag refresh (same as above) + verify the listing isn't buried by a title/tag mismatch |
| Favorites | 0 favorites by Day 4 with 50+ cumulative views | Image/price review: hero image or $24.99 price is not converting browsers — PM decides image swap vs promo depth |
| Conversion | 0 orders by Day 7 with 100+ cumulative views | Escalate to PM: extend/deepen the opening promo, or re-check price against #224's comp set (mid-cluster $20–$26 still holds) |
| Messages | Any buyer message unanswered >1 business day | Reply at once (per #194 templates); unanswered messages kill conversion |
| Reviews | No review 5 days after delivery | One follow-up message (per #194 tone: thank-you + gentle ask). Never send a second follow-up |

## Review follow-up timing
- **At delivery + 2 days:** if the buyer messaged positively, thank them and invite a review.
- **At delivery + 5 days:** if no review and no complaint, send ONE follow-up (template #194). Then stop — no nagging.
- **If a complaint arrives instead:** resolve the issue first (remake/refund per policy); never ask for a review on an unresolved order.

## What this checklist does NOT do
- It does not set an Etsy Ads budget (no pet-portrait Ads budget is on record — #198 was for family caricatures).
- It does not change the price or promo — those levers belong to the PM.
- It does not replace #227's launch-day checklist (pre-publish) — this starts the day the listing goes live.
