# Personalized-paddle returns handling one-pager (task #308)

**Product:** Custom birthday paddles · **Role:** engineer · **Date:** 2026-10-14
Per #60 shop policy. Response SLA: reply within 1 business day via Etsy Messages. Log every case in the order record.

## The non-resellable personalization rule
Every paddle is made to order with the buyer's name, age, photo, or custom text — it cannot be resold. **Physical paddles are final sale: no returns or exchanges for change of mind.** The flows below are the exceptions: misprint, damage, and lost packages. Never refuse a remedy in those cases by citing "final sale."

## Misprint
- **Our mistake** (we misspelled a name entered correctly, used the wrong colors/design, wrong photo): buyer chooses — **remake rushed and free, or full refund**. No return of the misprinted paddle needed.
- **Buyer's typo** (misspelled name in the personalization box, wrong photo uploaded): what the buyer typed is what we print. Buyer must message within **14 days of delivery**; offer a **remake at 50% off**. After 14 days, no remake obligation — seller's discretion.
- Spelling is confirmed at proof approval (per #306 workflow, PROPOSED) — after approval, a typo is the buyer's.

## Damage in transit
- Buyer reports **within 7 days of delivery** with a photo of the damage AND the packaging it came in.
- **Free replacement** — buyer does not send the damaged paddle back.
- If a replacement can't reach the buyer in time (e.g. the event date has passed): **full refund instead**.
- ⚠️ **Unresolved conflict:** #135 FAQ says 14 days for damage reports; #60 says 7 days. This one-pager follows #60 (the policy doc). PM must align #60 and #135 to one window before listing (per #131 FAIL 5 / #262 blocker 5).

## Lost package
- Tracking shows "delivered" but buyer has nothing → ask them to check neighbors, building mail area, porch first (mis-delivery / mis-scan is common). Wait 2 business days for mis-scans, then open a carrier inquiry.
- **No movement 10+ days past estimated delivery** OR **carrier confirms lost** → buyer chooses: **one free reship OR full refund**.
- One free reship max. A second loss → refund.
- If the buyer's event date has passed → refund, don't reship a late personalized paddle.
- File the carrier claim; keep the claim number in the order record.

## Not covered here
- **Cancellations:** full refund within 12 hours of purchase; after production starts, no cancellation (#60). ⚠️ #135 says "any time before production starts" — same PM alignment action as the damage window.
- **Etsy cases:** if the buyer opens a case, respond within Etsy's case window; never let a case go unanswered.
- **Chargebacks / second failures on the same order:** PM reviews before the next action.
