# Buyer message and review response templates — Custom birthday paddles (fleet task #187)
Product: Custom birthday paddles · Role: marketer · 2026-10-13
Placeholders: [NAME] buyer first name · [ORDER] order number · [DESIGN] design choice · [DATE] relevant date.
Tone: warm, plain-spoken, no groveling. Personalized items: no returns except our error — say it kindly every time it applies.

## 1. Photo problems

**Photo too small / blurry:**
Hi [NAME]! Thanks for sending your photo for order [ORDER]. The one you sent is a little too small to print crisply on the paddle — could you send the original full-size version (straight from your phone's camera roll, not a screenshot)? As soon as it lands I'll get your proof made up. Thanks!

**No photo received (photo variant ordered):**
Hi [NAME]! Just checking in on order [ORDER] — you chose the photo variant of the [DESIGN] paddle, but I don't see a photo in our messages yet. Reply here with one clear photo and I'll start your proof right away. (Tip: the original from your camera roll prints best!)

**Wrong photo / wants to swap before proof:**
No problem at all, [NAME] — I haven't started your proof yet, so send the right photo whenever you're ready and we'll use that one instead.

## 2. Proof approval nudges

**Proof sent (first message):**
Hi [NAME]! Your proof for order [ORDER] is attached 🎉 Please look it over carefully — check the spelling of the name, the age, and the photo placement. Reply "looks great" and I'll start production, or tell me what to change. Nothing gets made until you approve!

**Nudge 1 (3 days, no reply):**
Hi [NAME], just floating this back up — your paddle proof for order [ORDER] is waiting on your approval. Give it a look when you get a chance so we can keep things on schedule for the big day!

**Nudge 2 (7 days, no reply):**
Hi [NAME], last check-in on your proof for order [ORDER]. I can't start production without your OK, and I'd hate for it to arrive late. If I don't hear from you by [DATE], I'll go ahead and pause the order — just reply anytime to pick it back up.

## 3. Shipping questions

**When will it ship?**
Hi [NAME]! Your paddle is in production now. It ships [DATE RANGE], and I'll send the tracking number the moment it's on its way. If you need it by a specific party date, let me know and I'll confirm we're on track.

**Tracking / hasn't arrived:**
Hi [NAME], I checked the tracking for order [ORDER] — it's currently [STATUS/LICATION], estimated delivery [DATE]. USPS/UPS estimates can slip by a day or two; if it hasn't arrived by [DATE + 3 days], message me and I'll sort it out with the carrier.

**Need it by a party date (rush check):**
Hi [NAME]! I can see your party is [DATE]. Your order is scheduled to arrive [DATE] — that gives us [X] days of buffer. If anything slips in transit, message me immediately and we'll make it right.

## 4. Damage claims

**Arrived damaged:**
Oh no — I'm so sorry, [NAME]! Please send me a photo of the damage on order [ORDER] and I'll get a replacement made right away at no cost to you. That's on us, not you.

**Misprint / our error (wrong name, age, design):**
You're absolutely right, [NAME] — that's our mistake on order [ORDER]. I'm remaking your paddle correctly right now and I'll cover expedited shipping so it still arrives in time. So sorry about that.

**Buyer's remorse / wants to return (personalized, no error):**
Hi [NAME], I understand. Because each paddle is personalized and made to order just for you, I can't accept returns on items without an error — but if anything about yours isn't right (spelling, design, quality), send me a photo and I'll make it right immediately.

## 5. Review responses (post publicly on the review)

**5-star:**
Thank you so much, [NAME]! So glad the [DESIGN] paddle was a hit — hope the birthday was amazing! 🎉

**1-star (no error on our part):**
I'm sorry to hear this, [NAME]. We take every order personally — please message me directly about order [ORDER] so I can understand what went wrong and make it right.

**1-star (our error — after fixing):**
You're right, and I'm sorry — we got [SPECIFIC ERROR] wrong on your order. We've already [remade it / refunded you]. Thank you for giving us the chance to fix it.
