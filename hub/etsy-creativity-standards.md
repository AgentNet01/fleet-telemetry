# Etsy Creativity Standards: what we may list (summary)

Source: https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity (last updated Jun 10, 2025; read Oct 28, 2026 via Shop Manager's
Chrome). Summary in our words, not Etsy's text. Re-read the page before relying on an edge case.

Every item must fit one of four kinds:

1. **Made by a seller**: made by hand or with our own tools at home (laser cutter, 3D printer, Cricut).
   Not: assembling per instructions, or a mass-made item with a sticker added.
2. **Designed by a seller**: our original design, either as a digital download or produced by a
   **production partner** (e.g. Printify). Rules:
   - The listing must disclose the production partner and say truthfully where it ships from.
   - Anything made with AI must say so in the listing description.
   - Not: bundles or scans of someone else's work, AI prompt bundles.
3. **Handpicked by a seller**: vintage (20+ years old), items from nature, themed gift baskets of different
   items we picked. Not: resold new items, single-brand baskets.
4. **Sourced by a seller**: craft supplies, party decor, and items personalised with the buyer's own text
   or image, made by a production partner. Not: resold blanks, generic kitchen tools, everyday items, party
   favours or generic seasonal decor.

Photos: our own photos of the finished item. For personalised or engraved items the **first image must show a
finished, customised item**, not a blank or placeholder text. Commercial images are allowed only for
"sourced" craft/party supplies.

Etsy may remove a listing that breaks these rules and still charges its listing fee.

What this means for the hub:
- Resold AliExpress/CJ items fit none of the four: never on Etsy (`etsy.RESALE` refuses them).
- Printify items: production partner disclosed on the listing.
- AI-generated art (pet portraits, caricatures): "made with AI" stated in the description.
- Personalised items: first photo is a finished example.
