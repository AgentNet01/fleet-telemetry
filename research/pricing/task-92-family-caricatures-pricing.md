## Recommended price tiers

Digital delivery, no shipping cost, no Offsite Ads for pilot (same posture as other products). Etsy fees: **$0.45 fixed + 9.5% of item price** (listing $0.20 + transaction 6.5% + processing $0.25 + 3%).

| Tier | Price | Etsy fees | Net after fees | Margin vs price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Individual (1 person) | $34.99 | $3.77 | $31.22 | 89.2% |
| Couple (2 people) | $54.99 | $5.67 | $49.32 | 89.7% |
| Family group (3–4 people) | $79.99 | $8.05 | $71.94 | 89.9% |
| Each extra person (5+) | +$15.00 | $1.88 | $13.13 | 87.5% |

Fee math: $34.99 → 0.45 + 0.095×34.99 = $3.77. $54.99 → 0.45 + 5.22 = $5.67. $79.99 → 0.45 + 7.60 = $8.05. $15.00 → 0.45 + 1.43 = $1.88.

**Add-ons (recommended):** rush delivery (48h) +$10.00; pet counts as a person (market norm: +$22 observed); custom background +$15.00.

## Why these tiers (vs comparables)

- **Individual $34.99** sits between the discount tier ($9.50–$28) and the Star-Seller tier (€33.94–$40.00) — premium of the mid-market, under the $40 ceiling set by CartoonPortrait. Room to run 20% promos ($27.99) and still clear the discount shelf.
- **Couple $54.99** sits just above the observed $50.00 full price of the pencil-couple listing, justified by full-color family-caricature styling vs pencil; $20 above individual = the standard per-person step the market already teaches buyers.
- **Family $79.99 (3–4)** undercuts the premium ShakesCaricature ($260 for 5 + background) by a wide margin while pricing well above the $17–$36 discount cluster — the open mid-premium slot for 3–4 person families. $15/person beyond 4 keeps large families profitable without the $40/person shock of the top end.
- **Digital margins are fee-only:** with no COGS, margin before labor is ~89% at every tier. The binding constraint is production labor/time per portrait, not materials.

## What must be confirmed before launch

1. **Production cost per portrait** (agent/human time or tooling): margin above is gross before labor. If per-portrait production time exceeds ~30 min at target throughput, the individual tier is the first to break — consider $39.99.
2. **Delivery format decision:** digital download vs emailed proof workflow (the proof-before-delivery step is what the best shops sell on — CountryLazer/WoodAndSlateGifts pattern from the paddle scan).
3. **PM approval** of tiers, add-ons, and promo posture (recommend launch at full price with planned 20% holiday promos, matching shelf norms).
