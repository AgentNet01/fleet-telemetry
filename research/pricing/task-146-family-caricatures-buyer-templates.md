# Buyer message templates: family caricatures (task #146)

**Product:** Family caricatures · **Role:** marketer · **Date:** 2026-10-13
Status: TEMPLATES for shop use. Grounded in #90 (verified): photo requirements, revision policy (2 rounds included, $10 beyond), timeline (proof in 3 business days, 2 days per revision, final within 24h of approval, 48h rush add-on +$10).
Tone throughout: warm, unhurried, specific. Never blame the buyer's photo; always give the exact next step.

---

## 1. Low-res / unusable photo

**Tone:** encouraging, zero blame — the photo is "almost there," not "bad."

> Hi [name]! Thanks for sending these over — I can see exactly who I'm drawing, which is the fun part. One thing: the photo of [person] is a little blurry when I zoom in to catch the details (eyes, smile lines), and I want this to look great printed.
>
> Could you send one more of [person]? Ideal is: facing the camera, natural daylight (near a window beats flash), no sunglasses. A phone photo is totally fine — it just needs to be sharp when zoomed in.
>
> **Next step:** reply here with the new photo and I'll confirm within 24 hours, then your 3-business-day proof clock starts. No charge for the swap, and your place in the queue is held.

**Next step (internal):** hold the order open; start the proof clock only when a usable photo arrives.

## 2. Turnaround question ("how fast / will it arrive by [date]?")

**Tone:** precise and honest — give dates, not vibes.

> Great question! Here's the exact timeline once I have your photos:
> - **First proof:** within 3 business days
> - **Each revision round:** within 2 business days of your feedback
> - **Final files:** within 24 hours of your approval
>
> So if you send photos today ([date]), you'd see your first proof by [date+3bd]. In a hurry? Add the **rush option (+$10)** and the first proof lands within 48 hours.
>
> **Next step:** send your photos whenever you're ready — if you have a hard deadline (party, gift date), tell me the date and I'll confirm honestly whether we can hit it before you pay for rush.

**Next step (internal):** if the deadline can't be hit, say so plainly and offer a refund before work starts — a declined rush beats a 1-star review.

## 3. Revision request

**Tone:** collaborative — revisions are part of the craft, not a complaint.

> Love the notes — this is exactly what the proof round is for! Here's what I'm adjusting:
> - [change 1, e.g. bigger smile]
> - [change 2, e.g. warmer hair color]
>
> You'll have the revised proof within 2 business days. You have **2 revision rounds included** — this is round [1/2].
>
> Quick note: revisions cover likeness and style tweaks (expressions, colors, small details). A complete re-draw or adding a new person would be a separate order — happy to quote that if you'd like!
>
> **Next step:** sit tight — revised proof incoming by [date]. If you think of anything else, send it all in one message so we nail it in one round.

**Next step (internal):** if this is round 3+, quote $10 per additional round before starting it.

## 4. Rush order

**Tone:** can-do, with the boundary stated once and clearly.

> Absolutely — I can rush this! Here's how it works:
> - Add the **rush option (+$10)** at checkout (or I can send a custom listing for the add-on).
> - **First proof within 48 hours** of receiving your photos.
> - Revisions still run 2 business days per round, so send all feedback in one go to protect your timeline.
>
> **Next step:** purchase the rush add-on + send your photos now, and tell me your hard deadline — I'll confirm the full schedule (proof → revisions → final) against it before I start drawing.

**Next step (internal):** confirm the deadline is achievable with the 48h proof + revision buffers BEFORE accepting; decline honestly if not.

## 5. Gift order ("it's a surprise gift — can you…?")

**Tone:** conspiratorial and reassuring — you're in on the surprise.

> A surprise gift — my favorite kind of order! Here's how we keep it secret:
> - Everything happens here in Etsy messages, so nothing ships to them or shows up unexpectedly.
> - The final files come to **you** as a digital download — you print/frame and present it whenever you like.
> - If you need a gift-ready touch, I can add a **gift note line** on the artwork (e.g. "Merry Christmas 2026, love Emma") — just say the word in the personalization box.
>
> One tip: if the recipient might see your Etsy account, use a separate email for the order. And if you're short on time, the **rush option (+$10)** gets your first proof within 48 hours.
>
> **Next step:** place the order with the recipient's name/occasion in the personalization box, send the photos via message, and I'll take it from there — stealth mode on.

**Next step (internal):** never message anything gift-related to any address except the buyer's; double-check the personalization box for the gift line before finalizing.
