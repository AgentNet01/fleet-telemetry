# Launch promotion plan: family caricatures (task #145)

**Product:** Family caricatures · **Role:** marketer · **Date:** 2026-10-13
Status: PLAN for PM review. Nothing publishes or discounts without gate approval.
**Depends on:** #92 pricing tiers (in verification) — all price points below use the #92 proposal (Individual $34.99 / Couple $54.99 / Family $79.99 / +$15 per extra person; add-ons rush +$10, custom background +$15). If #92 changes, recompute the tables.

## Sale anchors (the permanent reference prices)

List prices are the anchors — shown struck through during every sale. Never launch already discounted (shelf norm: 30–75% off is common, so a stable anchor is what makes our 20–30% promos credible).

| Tier | Anchor (list price) |
|---|---|
| Individual (1 person) | $34.99 |
| Couple (2 people) | $54.99 |
| Family (3–4 people) | $79.99 |
| Each extra person (5+) | +$15.00 |

## Opening-sale coupon

- **Code:** `WELCOME20` — 20% off, minimum purchase $25, one use per customer.
- **Window:** launch day through day 7 (expires end of week 1).
- **Use:** Etsy coupon (Shop Manager > Marketing > Sales and coupons). Promote in the listing announcement, shop banner, and any social post.
- **Why a coupon, not just a sale:** coupons capture emails/favorites and feel exclusive; run it ALONGSIDE the week-1 sitewide 20% sale so first buyers get the deepest effective price and convert fast. First 10 sales + first reviews are the goal of week 1.

## Discount ladder (price points + margin per tier)

Etsy fees: $0.45 + 9.5% of the *sale* price. Digital product, no COGS — margin before labor stays ~89% at every discount level. Labor (production time per portrait) is the real constraint, unchanged by discounting.

| Tier | 20% off (standard promo) | 25% off (event) | 30% off (Black Friday max) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Individual | **$27.99** → fees $3.11 → net $24.88 | **$26.24** → fees $2.94 → net $23.30 | **$24.49** → fees $2.78 → net $21.71 |
| Couple | **$43.99** → fees $4.63 → net $39.36 | **$41.24** → fees $4.37 → net $36.87 | **$38.49** → fees $4.11 → net $34.38 |
| Family | **$63.99** → fees $6.53 → net $57.46 | **$59.99** → fees $6.15 → net $53.84 | **$55.99** → fees $5.77 → net $50.22 |

Rules: standard promos 20%, event promos 25%, **30% only for Black Friday week** — never deeper. Always a full-price week between sales (constant discounting trains buyers to wait and erodes the anchor).

## 30-day promo calendar (Oct 14 – Nov 12)

**Week 1 — LAUNCH (Oct 14–20): 20% off everything + WELCOME20 coupon.**
Goal: first 10 sales, first 5 reviews. Listing announcement + shop banner carry the coupon. Price points: $27.99 / $43.99 / $63.99.

**Week 2 — FULL PRICE (Oct 21–27): anchors hold.**
Content push: holiday gift-guide positioning ("a custom family portrait is the gift that can't be re-gifted"). Digital delivery = no shipping deadline, but the proof workflow means telling Christmas buyers to order by mid-December if they want it framed. Collect favorites/watchlists.

**Oct 30–31 — FALL PORTRAIT FLASH (48h, 25% off).**
Halloween-weekend traffic spike; angle is fall family portraits, not costumes. Price points: $26.24 / $41.24 / $59.99. Ends Nov 1, back to full price.

**Week 4 — FULL PRICE + HOLIDAY PUSH (Nov 4–12).**
Gift-occasion SEO: wedding, anniversary, Christmas. Announce the upcoming Black Friday week sale (Nov 23–30, 30% off — $24.49 / $38.49 / $55.99) in the shop announcement to build favorites; do NOT discount early.

**Next anchor after this window:** Black Friday week (Nov 23–30, 30% off), then full price through mid-December with an order-by-Dec-15 Christmas framing message. Christmas Day is Fri Dec 25; Thanksgiving Thu Nov 26.

## Etsy mechanics notes

- Use **Etsy Sales** (percentage off) for the sitewide events above — strikethrough pricing shows on the listing.
- Use **coupon codes** for targeted plays: cart-abandon follow-ups, social followers, repeat-buyer thank-yous (e.g., `THANKS10` after first review).
- Opt into Etsy's official sales events when they appear in Shop Manager > Marketing — event browse traffic is free.
- Track: conversion rate during sale vs full-price weeks, which tier discounts best, coupon redemption count. If the Individual tier doesn't move at 20% off, the problem is traffic/creative, not price — don't deepen the discount, fix the thumbnail.
