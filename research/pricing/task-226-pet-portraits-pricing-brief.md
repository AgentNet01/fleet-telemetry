# Pricing decision brief: pet portraits (task #226)

**Product:** Pet portraits (digital download) · **Role:** optimizer · **Date:** 2026-10-13
One page for the PM. Decision: **#44's $19.99 vs #139's $24.99.**

## Fee math and margin (digital — no COGS; margin before artist time)

Etsy fees: $0.45 + 9.5% of price. New shops can't opt out of Offsite Ads (15% when attributed) — shown as sensitivity.

| | **$19.99** (#44) | **$24.99** (#139) | Delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| Etsy fees | −$2.35 | −$2.82 | |
| **Net, organic** | **$17.64 (88.2%)** | **$22.17 (88.7%)** | **+$4.53 (+25.7%)** |
| Offsite-ads fee 15% | −$3.00 | −$3.75 | |
| **Net, offsite sale** | **$14.64 (73.2%)** | **$18.42 (73.7%)** | **+$3.78 (+25.8%)** |

At 20% promo: $19.99 → $15.99 (net $13.92); $24.99 → $19.99 (net $17.64).

## Market position (observed 2026-10-13)

Digital pet portraits cluster in three bands: budget $6–$12 (24h/quick-turn shops), **mid $18–$29** (Star-Seller multi-pet £18.30, hand-drawn watercolor $28.81), premium $38–$70. Both candidates sit in the mid-band: $19.99 = lower-mid, $24.99 = upper-mid.

## Recommendation: launch at **$24.99** with a 20% opening promo

1. **The $24.99 anchor buys positioning.** Upper-mid signals quality against the $6–$12 budget tier; $19.99 as a list price sits one discount away from budget territory.
2. **The promo does the $19.99 job.** 20% off $24.99 = $19.99 exactly — the strikethrough matches the shelf's permanent-sale norm, and the effective price still hits the impulse point #44 was aiming at.
3. **Headroom.** $24.99 leaves room for 25–30% event promos ($18.74–$17.49) without breaching a $15 net floor; $19.99 has nowhere to go but down into budget land.
4. **Risk is low either way.** Digital margins are ~88% at both prices; the binding constraint is conversion and reviews, not unit economics. If the listing doesn't convert at $24.99 in the first 30 days, drop the anchor to $19.99 — the promo mechanics make the move painless.

**If the PM prefers velocity over positioning:** $19.99 flat will convert faster for first-sale + first-reviews, at a cost of $4.53 net per sale and a weaker anchor. Legitimate choice if the goal is reviews in 14 days rather than margin.
